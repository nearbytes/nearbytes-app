# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors are expected to keep discussion technical, direct, and respectful.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- personal attacks
- harassment
- discrimination
- doxxing or threats
- deliberately disruptive review or issue behavior

## Project Norms

- criticize code and decisions, not people
- prefer concrete evidence over rhetoric
- surface risks and regressions clearly
- keep security disclosures private until maintainers respond

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or participants that violate these norms.
